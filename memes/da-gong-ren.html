'Dagongren': How Chinese Workers Turned Labor into a Rallying Cry

打工人 · da gong ren · ≈ “corporate slave / 9-to-5 warrior”

Dagongren, 'working person', is China's half-joking badge of wage labor — self-deprecating, unifying, and famous for 'Good morning, dagongren!'

Three-Layer Decoder

🔤 Symbol Layer▾

打工人 da gong ren

Literal translation: Hit-work-person

Structure: 'Da gong' (manual-labor work) + 'ren' (person): white-collar work downgraded to manual labor, used for self-mockery.

Emotional equivalent: “corporate slave / 9-to-5 warrior”(not literal — an emotionally equivalent phrase)

📖 Context Layer▾

Origin: A 2020 Bilibili creator's video went viral, sparking a wave of dagongren catchphrases.

Usage: Office workers mocking their own identity to defuse workplace pressure; 'Good morning, dagongren!' is the signature line.
