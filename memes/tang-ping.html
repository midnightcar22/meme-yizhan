Tangping (躺平) / Lying Flat: China's Quiet Protest Against the Rat Race

躺平 · tang ping · ≈ “lying flat / quiet quitting”

Tangping means 'lying flat' — opting out of the housing-and-overtime grind for a low-desire life, a gentle protest often misread as laziness.
