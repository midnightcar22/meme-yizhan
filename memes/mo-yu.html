摸鱼 mo yu

Literal translation: Fish-groping (moyu)

Structure: A clipped use of the idiom 'groping fish in muddy water': action + object only, inherently visual; originally meant profiting from chaos.

Emotional equivalent: “slacking off / goofing off at work”(not literal — an emotionally equivalent phrase)

📖 Context Layer▾

Origin: From the idiom 'groping fish in muddy water' (profiting from chaos); around 2020 its meaning drifted to 'slacking off at work'.

Usage: 'Slacking off' at work while appearing busy; 'moyu gadgets' = tools that help fake work; related to 'paid pooping' as workplace-resistance vocabulary.
