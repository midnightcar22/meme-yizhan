'Juejuezi': The Fandom Slang That Went from Praise to Sarcasm

绝绝子 · jue jue zi · ≈ “slay / iconic”

Born as fandom slang for 'absolutely iconic', juejuezi evolved into a deadpan way to say 'I can't even' — from gushing praise to sarcastic shade.

🔤 Symbol Layer▾

绝绝子 jue jue zi

Literal translation: Absolutely-ly-zi

Structure: 'Jue' (ultimate) reduplicated plus the classical suffix 'zi' — reduplication + suffix creates an exaggerated tone of praise.

Emotional equivalent: “slay / iconic”(not literal — an emotionally equivalent phrase)

📖 Context Layer▾

Origin: Fandom slang around 2021, from fans' exaggerated praise of idols on variety shows.

Usage: Expresses extreme praise — or, sarcastically, speechlessness; common in food and fandom contexts.
